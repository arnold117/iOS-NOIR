• 【实锤 / VERIFIED】：直接从 Apple 签名二进制 Entitlements、Info.plist 静态白名单或 HouseArrest 沙盒文件系统中提取到的确凿配置与暴露文件。
• 【机制实锤 / CAPABILITY】：应用持有 Apple 官方签发的特殊内核级豁免签名（如 unrestricted-voip-regulatory、HotspotHelper、multipath），具备随时绕过常规沙盒限制的底层能力（具体触发频率取决于服务端下发指令）。
• 【推断 / INFERRED】：基于共享 App Group 容器、Keychain 共享组及第三方风控/广告域名（如 shuzilm.cn、id6.me）的标准 SDK 行为推断其跨应用关联逻辑。

To avoid conflating static capability with active runtime abuse, every finding is graded into 3 forensic tiers with a plain-English "👉 What it does" explanation:
• [VERIFIED]: Directly extracted from Apple-signed Entitlements, Info.plist static whitelists, or unauthenticated HouseArrest sandbox enumeration.
• [CAPABILITY]: The app holds an Apple-signed privileged kernel entitlement (unrestricted-voip-regulatory, HotspotHelper, multipath) unlocking the bypass mechanism; trigger frequency depends on server policies.
• [INFERRED]: Cross-app identity correlation inferred from shared App Group containers, shared Keychain prefixes, and embedded attribution/risk SDK domains.

unrestricted-voip-regulatory 从 iOS 13 起苹果规定借道语音电话通道唤醒 App 必须弹出全屏来电界面，否则封杀；但持有该特批签名的超级 App（微信/QQ/钉钉），服务器随时可以下发无声指令把你彻底划掉的后台死进程拉活干活，手机屏幕不亮、不响、毫无痕迹。 Since iOS 13, Apple requires VoIP pushes to display a full-screen call UI. Apps holding this undocumented regulatory exemption (WeChat, QQ, DingTalk) can be silently woken from a dead state by their servers 10,000+ times without lighting up or ringing your screen.
HotspotHelper 本来是给连公共 Wi-Fi 工具用的接口，如果外卖/支付/社交软件持有它，只要你打开系统 Wi-Fi 列表或走过商场，iOS 内核就会自动唤醒它，把周围所有路由器的硬件 MAC 地址 (BSSID) 喂给它——哪怕你关了 GPS 定位，它也能反推出你在哪栋楼哪家店。 Intended for Wi-Fi portal utilities. When held by food-delivery or payment apps, iOS automatically wakes the app whenever Wi-Fi is scanned and feeds it nearby router MAC addresses (BSSIDs) — pinpointing your physical building/floor even with GPS turned off.
multipath / network-authentication 允许 App 在连着家里 Wi-Fi 或挂着 VPN 代理时，单独强行开启 4G/5G 蜂窝流量网卡直连移动/联通/电信网关，不仅瞬间暴露你的真实 SIM 卡归属地和基站 IP，还能配合运营商网关静默提取你的本机手机号。 Allows an app to force a 4G/5G cellular data connection (pdp_ip0) even while connected to Wi-Fi or a VPN, exposing your real carrier IP and enabling silent SIM phone-number extraction via telecom gateways.
ubiquity-kvstore-identifier 把唯一设备追踪码偷偷写进你的私人 iCloud 云盘隐藏空间。就算你卸载重装 App，甚至去苹果店买了一台全新 iPhone 登录同一个 Apple ID，它也能在后台秒级认出你还是原来那个人。 Writes unique tracking tokens into your private iCloud key-value storage. Even if you uninstall the app or buy a brand-new iPhone with the same Apple ID, the token automatically syncs back to re-identify you.
multicast 允许向你家里或公司的 Wi-Fi 发送多播探测包，摸清你家里装了什么牌子的电视、NAS 存储、智能音箱、打印机或路由器，用来精准评估你的家庭消费力与社会关系。 Sends mDNS/SSDP multicast discovery packets across your local Wi-Fi to enumerate smart TVs, NAS servers, speakers, and routers in your home.
LSApplicationQueriesSchemes 通过向系统挨个询问“能不能打开某个暗号（如 shadowrocket://、binance://、porsche://）”，在不弹窗的情况下摸清你手机里有没有装翻墙软件、境外银行、冷钱包或豪车车企 App。 Hardcodes up to 280+ URL schemes to silently query iOS (without any popup) whether you have VPN apps (shadowrocket), crypto wallets, offshore banks, or luxury car apps installed.
UIFileSharingEnabled / HouseArrest App 把内部 /Documents 文件夹直接暴露在外部同步接口下。只要手机插上信任过的电脑（或在同一局域网开启 Wi-Fi 同步），无需解锁 App 或验证 FaceID，1 秒钟就能直接列出甚至拷走里面的密码库、账单 PDF 与本地数据库。 Exposes the app's /Documents container over USB and local Wi-Fi sync. Any previously trusted computer can list and copy its files (.kdbx vaults, bank PDFs, SQLite logs) in 1 second without FaceID.
NSExceptionDomains 苹果默认强制所有请求走安全 HTTPS；如果开发者在配置里专门点名某些域名允许明文 HTTP 或忽略证书校验，顺藤摸瓜就能直接抓出它内部接入的第三方设备指纹库（如数字联盟 shuzilm.cn）、商业广告网（如秒针 miaozhen.com）甚至残留的热更新后门（jspatch.com）。 Domains where developers explicitly bypass Apple's HTTPS App Transport Security — directly exposing embedded device-DNA SDKs (shuzilm.cn), ad-tech trackers (miaozhen.com), or hot-patching backdoors (jspatch.com).

⚖️ 方法论局限与严谨性边界（必读） ⚖️ Methodology Limitations & Forensic Boundaries

  1. 未越狱 FairPlay 壳限制：未越狱设备上的 App Store 二进制受 Apple FairPlay DRM 硬件加密保护，本工具不进行脱壳或 Ghidra 反编译；如需针对单款已脱壳 IPA 进行函数级反编译与字符串交叉引用，请参考并结合 RootSir/claude-ios-privacy-analysis 的 Ghidra 自动化管线。
  2. 特权存在性【机制实锤】 ≠ 全天候触发【实锤】：应用持有 unrestricted-voip-regulatory 或 HotspotHelper 证明苹果官方为其签发了突破常规限制的底层准入证，但是否在特定时刻下发静默唤醒或上报 BSSID 取决于服务端指令与运行时逻辑。
  3. ATS 豁免域名 ≠ 当前活跃抓包流量：NSExceptionDomains 中的域名（如 jspatch.com、shuzilm.cn）为开发者在 Info.plist 中显式声明的白名单，部分可能是历史遗留配置或按条件触发的备用通道。
  1. FairPlay DRM Boundary on Stock iOS: App Store binaries on non-jailbroken devices are FairPlay-encrypted. iOS-NOIR audits whole-device entitlements, plists, sandbox containers, and kernel telemetry without decrypting Mach-O code sections. For function-level Ghidra decompilation of decrypted IPAs, combine with RootSir/claude-ios-privacy-analysis.
  2. Capability ([CAPABILITY]) ≠ Continuous Runtime Trigger ([VERIFIED]): Holding an Apple-signed entitlement proves the kernel bypass is unlocked, whereas actual trigger frequency depends on server-side push commands.
  3. Static ATS Domains ≠ Live Traffic: Domains in NSExceptionDomains are statically declared developer exceptions in Info.plist; some may be legacy or conditional endpoints.
